Speaking & media

Unsettled ocean rules, made clear to a room.

Sarah M. Reiter has spoken at TEDxBoston and facilitated a session on the future of shipping at an International Maritime Organization and World Maritime University event in Sweden. Reiter has also run simulated High Seas Treaty (BBNJ) negotiations at Oxford, Dartmouth and Vermont Law School, showing audiences how treaty rules get made.
