Shipping · Arctic

Is sea ice land, water or neither?

The question. The International Maritime Organization (IMO) was negotiating a Polar Code for ice-covered waters. No treaty settles what sea ice is in law.

The work. As a legal intern in the International Section of NOAA's Office of General Counsel (2012), Reiter drafted language on the legal status of sea ice for U.S. interagency negotiations on the Polar Code. She also co-authored a comparison of U.S. and Canadian law on the question (Polar Geography, 2013).

Why it matters now. New ocean technology raises the same problem: what an object is in law decides which rules apply.

Deep-sea minerals

Protecting the seabed before mining begins

The question. How should the International Seabed Authority (ISA) protect parts of the Clarion-Clipperton Zone while writing mining rules?

The work. Reiter's research contributed to ISA deliberations on the zone's Areas of Particular Environmental Interest. She also co-authored "Managing mining of the deep seabed" (Science, 2015).

Why it matters now. The Mining Code is unfinished, and a U.S. permitting track now runs alongside it.

Environmental review · High seas

Counting cumulative impacts

The question. Environmental reviews assess projects one at a time and miss their combined stress.

The work. Reiter co-authored studies of cumulative impacts in U.S. environmental review (Stanford Environmental Law Journal, 2014) and in California's marine protected areas (Ocean & Coastal Management, 2017). Her cumulative-impacts research informed the High Seas Treaty (BBNJ) negotiations.

Why it matters now. New ocean technology deploys in fleets and arrays, so the combined effect is the case a reviewer will test.

Climate diplomacy

Ocean commitments under the Paris Agreement

Reiter led Vermont Law School's observer delegation to the UN climate negotiations, in support of Angelique Pouponneau, then CEO of the Seychelles Conservation and Climate Adaptation Trust. She was lead author of a framework for operationalizing climate-just ocean commitments under the Paris Agreement (Frontiers in Climate, 2021).

Advisory · Upwelling Consulting

Prior consulting practice

As co-owner of Upwelling Consulting, LLC (2019–2022), Reiter analyzed climate-adaptation legislation and grant criteria, southern sea otter recovery, and international law on whale capture and captivity.
