Advisory services

03

International ocean governance

For anyone operating beyond national waters

The High Seas Treaty is in force; autonomous-ship and seabed-mining rules are not settled. I read the negotiating texts against your operations.

  • Briefings on BBNJ, IMO and ISA processes
  • Readiness review for BBNJ impact assessment
  • Draft comments and submissions
