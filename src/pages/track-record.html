Shipping · Arctic

Is sea ice land, water or neither?

No treaty settles it, and the IMO was writing a Polar Code. As a legal intern at NOAA's Office of General Counsel (2012), Reiter drafted language on the legal status of sea ice for U.S. interagency negotiations, and co-authored a comparison of U.S. and Canadian law (Polar Geography, 2013).

Deep-sea minerals

Protecting the seabed before mining begins

Reiter's research contributed to International Seabed Authority deliberations on protected areas in the Clarion-Clipperton Zone. She co-authored "Managing mining of the deep seabed" (Science, 2015).

Environmental review · High seas

Counting cumulative impacts

Reviews assess projects one at a time and miss their combined stress. Reiter co-authored studies of cumulative impacts in U.S. environmental review (Stanford Environmental Law Journal, 2014) and California's marine protected areas (2017). That line of research informed the High Seas Treaty negotiations.

Climate diplomacy

Ocean commitments under the Paris Agreement

Reiter led Vermont Law School's observer delegation to the UN climate negotiations, in support of Angelique Pouponneau, then CEO of the Seychelles Conservation and Climate Adaptation Trust, and was lead author of a framework for climate-just ocean commitments (Frontiers in Climate, 2021).

Advisory · Upwelling Consulting

Prior consulting practice

As co-owner of Upwelling Consulting, LLC (2019–2022), Reiter advised on climate-adaptation legislation, southern sea otter recovery, and international law on whale captivity.
