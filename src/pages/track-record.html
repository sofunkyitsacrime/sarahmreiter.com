Track record

Where clear science moved a legal or policy decision.

Selected work across two decades. Client advisory matters are described without identifying details.

Shipping · Arctic

Is sea ice land, water or neither?

The question: As the IMO negotiated a mandatory Polar Code for ships in ice-covered waters, U.S. negotiators needed a defensible position on the legal status of sea ice, which no treaty settles.

The work: At NOAA's Office of General Counsel, Sarah drafted language on the legal status of sea ice for interagency negotiations, building on her own peer-reviewed comparison of U.S. and Canadian law (Polar Geography).

Why it matters now: Frontier technologies raise the same kind of question: what an object is in law decides which rules apply to it.

Deep-sea minerals

Protecting the seabed before mining begins

The question: How should the International Seabed Authority set aside protected areas in the Clarion-Clipperton Zone while it writes rules for commercial mining?

The work: As a co-author of "Managing mining of the deep seabed" in Science (2015), Sarah contributed research that fed into ISA deliberations on Areas of Particular Environmental Interest.

High seas · Environmental review

Counting cumulative impacts

The question: Environmental review documents routinely assess projects one at a time, missing the combined stress on ocean ecosystems.

The work: Sarah co-authored a major analysis of cumulative impacts in U.S. environmental review (Stanford Environmental Law Journal, 2014) and an assessment of California's marine protected area network. That research went on to inform negotiations of the BBNJ Agreement.

Shipping · Climate

Shipping at the biodiversity–climate nexus

The work: Facilitated and presented work on future shipping measures that address biodiversity and climate together, convened by the International Maritime Organization and World Maritime University (2024).

Seafood · Standards

Science to action, measured

The work: Co-developed and launched a $15M+, five-department science-to-action initiative at the New England Aquarium, with outcomes set first and measured throughout.

Outcomes: Influence over more than 2 million metric tons of seafood; sustainability evaluations of 25+ wild and farmed species; influence on three global fisheries and aquaculture standards.

Advisory · Upwelling Consulting

Prior consulting practice

As co-owner of Upwelling Consulting, LLC (2019–2022), Sarah analyzed federal and state climate-adaptation legislation and funding criteria. She also ran stakeholder, legal and advocacy analysis for southern sea otter recovery, and comparative international legal analysis on the capture and captivity of whales.

Climate diplomacy

Ocean commitments under the Paris Agreement

Served as Head of Observer Delegation to the UN climate negotiations supporting the Seychelles Conservation and Climate Adaptation Trust, and co-authored a framework for putting ocean commitments under the Paris Agreement into practice (Frontiers in Climate, 2021).
